Up and running in three steps.

Keep the downloaded files together. Your personal settings are created locally on first run.

01 / DOWNLOAD

Get the ZIP

Download the Windows build below and extract the entire JARVIS folder somewhere you can write to, such as Documents or Downloads.

02 / LAUNCH

Open JARVIS

Double click JARVIS.exe inside the extracted folder. Keep it with the other files; do not move the executable out on its own.

03 / CONFIGURE

Choose your AI

Enter your own Gemini API key at first run, or configure a supported local engine in the app. Connect a microphone, then start talking or type a command.
